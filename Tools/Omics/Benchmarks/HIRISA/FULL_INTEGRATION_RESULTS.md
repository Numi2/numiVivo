## Independent checks and response scope

The frozen NumPy checker reads every explicit matrix coordinate/value in bounded
8,192-row batches, verifies every donor assignment against original metadata,
and independently reconstructs the final objective and ridge correction.
Maximum membership row-sum error is **2.55351e-15**; maximum corrected-coordinate
discrepancy is **2.34222e-11**. The independent final objective is
-169.16495083036153, within the previously frozen mixed tolerance of the native
-169.16495081710087. The check took 6.90 seconds with 165,052,416 bytes maximum
RSS. It verifies numerical consistency, not biological truth or the complete
stochastic optimization trajectory. Native replay supplies the separate exact
reconstruction gate.

All sixteen contrasts pass the [frozen response margins](INTEGRATION_RESPONSE.md)
across the original 79 donor-held-out folds. Maximum contrast-mean accuracy loss
is 0.002546 and maximum individual-fold loss 0.019370. Mean paired-response
relative drift is 0.004105–0.138859. All 23 measured enrichment/treatment strata
show lower donor-associated variance fractions; their unweighted mean falls
from 0.079429 to 0.020288. That quantity includes confounded donor/batch variation
and does not isolate technical batch removal.

The classifier evaluates 1,275,710 distinct matched cells; all other 336,884
original cells remain in descriptive moments/scatter. Four classification
controls remain insufficient near baseline chance. The mean-response erasure
control detects loss in all sixteen contrasts. Full-cohort preprocessing is
transductive, experimental enrichment is not verified cell identity, and author
predictions remain nonauthoritative. Marker/program and rare-cell preservation,
native multi-seed robustness and broader biological validation are still open.
[Three full Harmony references](HARMONY_REFERENCE.md) also pass these same coarse
metrics; their complete comparison is recorded separately.
