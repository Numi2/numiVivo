# Intervention design — v0.4 development increment

The lab now seals a molecular objective and every eligible intervention together,
reveals their measured responses, and evaluates the **choice** against uniform
random selection, a training-response ranking, and no intervention. Native
target-aware models were trained and evaluated across public studies. A second,
bounded cross-assay experiment was executed after the first failure.

**The requested v0.4 milestone is not complete. Biological promotion is not
earned.** The neural target representation has not demonstrated useful selection
advantage. A measured spatial preservation campaign, verified spatial-edge
ablations, and Safari qualification remain unavailable. Passing replay does not
change those outcomes. v0.2 and v0.3 results are retained unchanged.

## What ran

The first campaign trains on the Adamson K562 pilot (GSE90546/GSM2406675) and
Dixit K562 high-MOI study (GSE90063). Only unique single-guide assignments are
admitted from the high-MOI source. CRISPR repression and knockout have distinct
inputs. There are 17 training source-target groups, represented by 136 fixed
resampling bags; these bags are **not biological replicates**. GSE274058 batch B2
provides 13 validation groups. B1 fails the explicit-control count gate, so there
is no mouse response training in this campaign. All exclusions remain recorded.

GSE92872 (Jurkat stimulated and unstimulated) and GSE274058 B3 were reserved
before response extraction. The initial campaign has 79 reserved target-context
groups on a 519-gene axis. Human-cell-context transfer and mouse-tissue transfer
are reported separately. Unseen target names are retained in the prediction seal;
the resulting scores are not one pooled zero-shot claim.

After these outcomes were exposed, a follow-up preregistration admitted the
previously exposed Jurkat 2017 data into training. Captures 1–5 are training and
capture 6 is development checkpoint selection, **not independent biological
validation**. GSE168620/GSM5151370, a 2021 scifi RNA-seq Jurkat experiment, was
newly reserved. This is cross-platform transfer in the same cell-line context,
not unseen-cell-context validation. Training uses three studies, 648 bags;
validation has 31 groups and evaluation 40 groups on 515 genes. Shared cell-line
ancestry and unresolved culture-level overlap prevent a claim of independently
replicated biology.

The original four-gene program is FOS/JUN/JUNB/EGR1. JUNB is absent from the
follow-up feature axis. The full objective remains unavailable. Before opening
the follow-up responses, `followup-objective-admission.json` froze the separately
labelled FOS/JUN/EGR1 objective. Its scores are not directly comparable to the
original four-gene scores. Neither thresholds nor earlier verdicts were changed.

The 149-dimensional target representation consists of source-bound UniProt
protein amino-acid composition, log sequence length, and hashed GO annotations.
Species, intervention modality and stimulation context are separate channels.
Mouse targets use pinned one-to-one MGI human orthology; this is an explicit
cross-species assumption. Dose and time are not fabricated where metadata do not
support them. No expression-pretrained weights are used. Raw source hashes and
disjoint row filters are checked. Annotation knowledge can contain prior
experimental literature; exhaustive independence from all annotation literature
has not been established.

All learned variants reuse `VivoCellResponseMLXModel`, width 64, with the fixed
240/720/1440 checkpoint budget. Principal target conditioning uses the biological
descriptor, not a masked unknown target ID. Matched native comparisons use target
ID or shuffled biological descriptors. A regularized descriptor/PCA model and
transparent GO-neighbor response transfer provide simpler comparisons. The latter
uses the gene-relationship principle; it is **not a GEARS reproduction**. The
earlier State comparison remains **PerturbMean, not State Transition**.

## Selection outcomes

Utility is minus the mean intervention-induced log1p(CPM) change in the declared
program. Higher is better. No intervention is an explicit zero-change option.
All eligible targets stay in the evaluation. Ties are lexical, as preregistered;
a no-change *predictor* can therefore select an arbitrary tied target. Its choice
score is distinct from the explicit no-intervention option.

| Reserved context | Native descriptor choice | Gain over random | Gain over training ranking | Regret to best measured eligible |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jurkat 2017 stimulated | GATA3 | +0.07697 | +0.10574 | 0.34452 |
| Jurkat 2017 unstimulated | NFAT5 | -0.2790 | retained in record | 0.5772 |
| Mouse B3 | LRRK2 | -0.2139 | retained in record | 1.3131 |
| Jurkat 2021 stimulated | EGR1 | -0.005648 | -0.023049 | 0.025570 |
| Jurkat 2021 unstimulated | EGR1 | -0.0036 | -0.0107 | retained in record |

The initial training-mean comparator is target-agnostic within modality. The
follow-up explicitly strengthens it to target/context-matched training response
where available, otherwise modality mean. Original scores are not rewritten.
In the original stimulated context, shuffled target descriptors select a better
observed candidate than the biological descriptors. In both follow-up contexts,
the simple descriptor model and GO transfer match the training ranking and beat
the neural choice. The isolated positive original point result does not establish
use of transferable target biology. No reliable winner is displayed.

`evidence/v04` retains all model/target comparisons, endpoint and response RMSE,
direction, magnitude, marginal Gaussian cross-entropy, selection scores and
budget-3 retrospective acquisition. Disagreement, least-covered acquisition and
1,000 seeded uniform-random selections are compared without a claim of calibrated
information gain or active-learning benefit. Population-mean sampling error,
cell dispersion and transfer uncertainty remain different quantities. There are
too few independently resolved evaluation units for biological intervals.

## Receiving-population experiment

The exposed GSE274447 spatial corpus remains development evidence. The receiver
experiment separates target × role × uncertain receiving type, supplies each
receiving group's own projected reference RNA, and excludes detected coexposure
to another target. Barcode-negative receiving cells are not called untreated.
There are 54 training, 33 validation and 26 development-comparison groups.

| Fixed-geometry development model | Mean group RMSE |
|---|---:|
| Original anchor reference, pretrained response | 0.778919 |
| Receiver reference, spatial-only training | 0.922937 |
| Receiver reference, pretrained response | 0.930173 |
| Receiver-reference no change | 0.918330 |

These are matched endpoints on the common measured gene axis. This configuration
does not support either the receiver-reference improvement or a pretraining
benefit. It does not prove receiver conditioning is intrinsically unhelpful.
References are sparse and labels remain uncertain projections. The earlier
case-sensitive guide-label and missing-feature-mask attempts are retained as
superseded/invalid development attempts; final scoring uses source masks.

Individual cells are not bound to resolved sections in the available source.
Original 15-nearest chip-coordinate outcome associations remain development
hypotheses. **No verified-edge or edge-shuffling model was executed.** Distances
are uncalibrated chip coordinates, not microns. No computation step denotes time,
and no weight or attention value is a biological mechanism.

GSE274058's GEO description (five mice) and pooled-experiment description remain
in conflict; splitting uses conservative batch blocks. The paper's 247-gene
Xenium experiment is not admitted without raw counts, guide assignments and an
animal/section crosswalk. Neither a different file nor a different assay proves
independence. A selected-population objective with measured neighboring-population
preservation therefore cannot yet execute on newly reserved spatial evidence.

## What the failures discriminate

Development-only oracle metric controls distinguish correct identity, wrong identity,
wrong magnitude, opposite response, generic mean and zero. Magnitude alone cannot
detect a sign reversal; direction and selection regret are retained alongside it.
This does not retrospectively revise v0.3.

The native descriptor model nearly collapses target differences: original
stimulated-program predicted between-target SD is 0.0000129 versus observed
0.2270. This is also a **training-fit limitation**: after averaging resampling bags by source/context/target, mean feature-wise
response SD is 0.00390 predicted versus 0.15694 observed in the original 17
groups, and 0.00422 versus 0.40325 in the follow-up 81 groups. Group response RMSE
only slightly improves over no change (0.16196 vs 0.16959; 0.40534 vs 0.41206).
The groups and resampling bags are not independent biological replicates. Therefore cross-study mismatch
alone is not a sufficient explanation. Activation-scale diagnostics are retained
as numerical observations, not a demonstrated causal explanation.

The follow-up also has weak measurement of this objective: across 39,194 cells,
FOS is detected in 20, JUN in 64 and EGR1 in 198. The program's control detection
fractions are retained by context. Split-cell agreement describes sampling noise,
not an independent biological accuracy limit. More cells from the same shallow
assay would not establish reproducibility or rescue the original failed result.

The next discriminating model experiment is a **development-only**, fixed-size
optimization/response-scaling comparison that must first recover target-specific
training responses and pass wrong-target controls. The next independent biological
test needs an objective with prespecified control-reference detection support,
separate resolved biological replicates, and adequate measurement depth. A spatial
preservation test additionally needs verified section identities and receiver
controls. These requirements follow the measured failure modes; no additional
evaluation outcomes have been tuned against after the follow-up reveal.

## Reproduce and inspect

The retained local evidence root is `/Users/home/virtual-wet-lab-v04-20261004`.
Use the scientific Python environment with anndata, scipy, pandas, scikit-learn,
requests and safetensors. Source manifests contain download identities and hashes.

```sh
python Tools/VirtualWetLab/prepare_intervention_design.py --root EVIDENCE --output NEW_INPUTS
python Tools/VirtualWetLab/train_intervention_design.py --inputs NEW_INPUTS --binary NATIVE_NUMIVIVO --output NEW_CAMPAIGN
python Tools/VirtualWetLab/train_receiving_populations.py --inputs EVIDENCE/learning-v3 --campaign EVIDENCE/campaign-v1 --binary NATIVE_NUMIVIVO --output NEW_RECEIVER_RUN
python Tools/VirtualWetLab/check_intervention_design.py --campaign EVIDENCE/campaign-v1 --workspace NEW_CHECKS --output CHECK.json
```

Run preparation/training again with `--root EVIDENCE/followup` for the follow-up
manifest. Fresh reruns are reproductions of now-exposed data, not new validation.
The fixed v0.3 source cohort and pinned orthology table must be present at their
retained paths for source rebuilding. Native warm start resets optimizer and
sampler; it is adaptation, not checkpoint resume.

NumiLab's existing `virtual_wet_lab.py` accepts `--design-campaign CAMPAIGN` and
`--receiving-campaign RECEIVER_RUN` alongside existing assays. The browser links
candidate objectives, reference/predicted/measured gene values, computed choice
diagnostics and exact native replay. Separate receiver distributions reuse the
existing tissue canvas and signed shared-scale views. Unsupported preservation
is visibly disabled. Packaged cohorts are labelled retrospective after reveal.

Fifteen existing contract tests pass. Lifecycle qualification rejects changed
objectives, prohibits unsupported preservation, keeps observations unavailable
before seal, recovers after an interrupted observation snapshot without rereading
source RNA, and replays all native means/variances bit-exactly. Receiver replay
and v0.3 lifecycle regression pass; the v0.2 molecular record still verifies.
These checks do not qualify the biological model.

On the actual Mac16,12 with 24 GB memory, process-cold Chromium workspace readiness
was 2.532 s; full import of 123,921 cells was 7.055 s. Candidate preview/seal/replay
requests were 45/152/729 ms, reveal 5.584 s. The filesystem cache was not cleared.
Cached instrumented picking/drawing are recorded separately. **Safari did not run**:
remote automation is disabled and native UI access failed. First-launch browser
and interaction checks are scoped to this Mac and browser, not general hardware.
The full software release gate therefore remains incomplete.

The development archive retains native runtime/Metal bundle, nine selected
models and their other fixed-budget checkpoints, training inputs, sealed records,
source metadata, target annotations, all results and browser evidence. Portable
native query replay does not require absolute source paths. Full source
re-extraction does; historical records are never rewritten to make them portable.
